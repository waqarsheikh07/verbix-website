Our standards

Your work stays yours.

Verbix provides editing, feedback and guidance. This page sets out exactly what that means.

We do

  • Correct grammar, spelling and punctuation
  • Improve clarity, flow and academic tone
  • Comment on structure and argument
  • Check citations and formatting
  • Explain our changes so you can learn

We don’t

  • Write essays, assignments or chapters
  • Add new ideas, arguments or research
  • Take exams or online tests
  • Help hide copied work from similarity checks
  • Share your files with anyone

Following your university’s rules

Many universities publish guidance on proofreading by a third party. Our editing is designed to stay within those limits. If your institution restricts editing, tell us and we will work within that, or tell you honestly if we cannot help.

Similarity reports

When we review a similarity report, we show you where quotes need marks, paraphrases need rewording in your own words, and sources need citing. The goal is honest, correctly referenced work.

Privacy

Files are stored only as long as your order needs them and deleted on request. We never use client work as samples. The excerpts we send on request come from consented or self-produced material.
