The editor

Six years in academic writing

Our founder has spent six years helping students and researchers present their work clearly, across business, nursing, computing, engineering and the social sciences.

That experience means knowing what markers look for: a clear argument, evidence used well, consistent referencing and English that never gets in the way of the ideas.

Every Verbix order is handled personally. You will know who is reading your work, and you can ask them questions directly.

What we will never do

We do not write assignments, take exams or produce work for you to submit as your own. Your ideas and arguments stay yours. Read our standards for the full policy.

What you can count on

Your voice stays

We edit to make you sound like the best version of yourself, not like us.

Every change explained

Tracked changes and margin comments, so you learn as well as improve.

Private by default

Straight answers

A real person replies on WhatsApp or email, usually within 2 hours.
